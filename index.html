Squeezed Day

A day view for your calendar that keeps every appointment readable and folds long empty stretches into thin strips, so more of your day fits on the screen.

Add your calendar

Choose a calendar file (it ends in .ics, or .zip if it came from Google Calendar). It's read on this device and never uploaded.

Many calendars only let you save this file from their website on a computer, not from the phone app. The file is a copy: later changes need a fresh one.

Where do I get a calendar file?

Most calendar apps can save a copy of your calendar as a file, but many only let you do it from their website on a computer, not from the phone app. Save it there, send it to your phone (for example by emailing it to yourself), then add it here.

It's a copy. Changes you make in your calendar afterwards won't show up here. When you want them, save a fresh copy and add it again.

  • Google Calendar: on a computer, open Google Calendar, click the gear, then Settings, Import & export, and Export. You get a .zip file with all your calendars; add it here as it is. For just one calendar, open that calendar's Settings and sharing and choose Export calendar.
  • Apple Calendar on a Mac: click the calendar in the list on the left, then choose File, Export, Export.
  • Outlook and other apps: look for Export or Save calendar in the app's menus or settings, and pick the iCalendar (.ics) format if asked.
